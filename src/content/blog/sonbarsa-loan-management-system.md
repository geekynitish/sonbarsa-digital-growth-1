## Lending Runs on Spreadsheets Longer Than It Should

Most small and mid-sized lenders — NBFCs, cooperative societies, local finance companies — still run loan books across a mix of spreadsheets, paper files, and disconnected tools. It works until the book grows past a few hundred active loans, at which point approvals slow down, EMI dates get missed, and overdue accounts fall through the cracks. Sonbarsa built its Loan Management System (LMS) to replace that patchwork with a single system that covers the full loan lifecycle.

### 1. Loan Application and Approval

Borrower details, documents, and eligibility checks are captured digitally at the point of application. Approval workflows route each case through the right sign-off levels automatically, so underwriting decisions are consistent and auditable instead of dependent on one person's memory.

### 2. Disbursement Tracking

Once approved, disbursement is logged against the loan record — amount, date, mode, and any partial-disbursement schedule — so there's a single source of truth for what was actually paid out, not just what was sanctioned.

### 3. EMI Scheduling That Matches the Loan Type

- Auto-generated EMI schedules for car loans, home loans, and bike loans, each with their own tenure and interest structures
- Support for step-up, bullet, and standard EMI patterns
- Automatic recalculation when a borrower prepays or restructures a loan

### 4. Repayment and Overdue Tracking

Every repayment updates the loan ledger in real time, and accounts that miss a due date are flagged automatically — no manual reconciliation needed to know which loans have gone overdue and by how many days.

### 5. Built for Branch and NBFC Operations

The system is designed for the way Indian lending businesses actually operate: multiple branches, multiple loan products, and staff who need role-based access rather than one shared login for everyone.
