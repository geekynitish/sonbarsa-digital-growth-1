## Core Modules of the Sonbarsa Finance Management Tool

The Sonbarsa Finance SaaS ecosystem integrates every critical capability required by modern financial institutions:

### 1. Sonbarsa Loan Management Tool (End-to-End Lending Lifecycle)

The **Sonbarsa Loan Management Tool** automates the complete loan journey from borrower onboarding to final closure:
- **Instant Digital Onboarding:** Capture borrower details, e-KYC, Aadhaar, PAN, and bank statements digitally via mobile or desktop apps.
- **Smart Disbursement Engine:** Supports instant single and multi-tranche disbursements directly to borrower accounts with automated bank verification.
- **Dynamic EMI Schedules:** Configurable schedules supporting standard EMI, step-up, bullet repayment, daily/weekly microfinance collections, and custom interest algorithms.
- **Prepayment & Restructuring:** Real-time ledger updates when borrowers prepay, foreclosure calculations, or request loan restructuring.

### 2. Agentic AI & Autonomous Credit Risk Underwriting

Sonbarsa Finance integrates state-of-the-art **Agentic AI workflows** to evaluate borrower risk in seconds rather than days:
- **Predictive Default Scoring:** Machine Learning (MLOps) models analyze credit bureau data (CIBIL, Experian, Crif High Mark), account aggregator statements, and transactional telemetry to output instant credit scores.
- **Automated Fraud Detection:** Computer Vision algorithms verify identity documents and flag anomalies in income proof or bank statements before sanctioning.
- **Rule-Based Decision Engine:** Auto-approve low-risk applications and route complex cases to underwriting committees with pre-calculated risk profiles.

### 3. Sonbarsa Collection and Recovery Tool (Smart Debt Recovery)

Delinquency management is where lending profitability is won or lost. The **Sonbarsa Collection and Recovery Tool** transforms debt management:
- **AI-Powered Debt Bucketing:** Categorizes loans into SMA-0, SMA-1, SMA-2, and NPA buckets in real time.
- **Autonomous WhatsApp & Voice AI Agents:** Interactive conversational AI bots automatically send personalized payment reminders, collect payment promises, and send direct UPI/Razorpay payment links.
- **Field Force & Officer Management:** Geo-tagged mobile apps for collection agents with live route optimization, receipt generation, and offline sync.

### 4. Sonbarsa Finance CRM (Customer Lifecycle & Retention)

Retaining good borrowers is significantly more profitable than acquiring new ones. The **Sonbarsa Finance CRM** module provides:
- **360-Degree Borrower Profile:** Complete historical view of loan history, repayment behavior, communication logs, and customer support tickets.
- **Automated Top-Up & Cross-Sell:** AI models identify prompt-paying borrowers eligible for pre-approved top-up loans or secondary credit facilities.
- **Omnichannel Support:** Integrated SMS, email, WhatsApp, and voice communications inside one unified dashboard.
