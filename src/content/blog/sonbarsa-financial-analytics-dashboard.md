## Lending Decisions Are Only as Good as the Numbers Behind Them

Branch managers and leadership at lending businesses often make decisions on numbers that are a week old, pulled together manually from multiple registers before a review meeting. Sonbarsa's Financial Analytics Dashboard replaces that with live numbers pulled directly from the loan book, sales pipeline, and collection activity.

### 1. Portfolio and Revenue at a Glance

Total book size, disbursement trends, and revenue by loan product are visible in one place, updated as new loans are sanctioned and disbursed rather than reconstructed at month-end.

### 2. NPA and Overdue Tracking

- Overdue accounts broken down by days-past-due bucket
- NPA trends tracked over time, not just as a single point-in-time snapshot
- Early-warning visibility into accounts drifting toward default, before they become write-offs

### 3. Collection Efficiency, Measured Properly

Collection efficiency is tracked against amount due, not just amount collected, so teams can see whether recovery effort is actually closing the gap or just staying busy.

### 4. Branch and Agent Performance

Disbursement, overdue, and collection numbers are broken down by branch and by individual agent, giving managers a fair basis for performance conversations instead of relying on impressions.

### 5. Built for Review Meetings, Not Just Record-Keeping

Reports are structured around the questions leadership actually asks in a review — what's the book size, what's overdue, what's being recovered — instead of raw exports that need to be reshaped before anyone can use them.
